鲸舱 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 26 日

鲸舱是一款面向企业内部员工的工单与网格管理工具，仅供获得企业授权的员工使用。本政策说明我们收集哪些信息、为什么收集，以及如何处理。

一、我们收集的信息

1. 位置信息（精确位置）

  • 何时收集：你使用地图页、查看附近门店、执行工单签到，或作为网格员开启位置共享时。
  • 为什么：在地图上展示你的位置、按位置筛选门店与工单、记录到访时间轴、为导航算路。
  • 是否后台收集：仅限 App 内导航进行中。你在导航途中切到其他应用或锁屏时，本应用会继续获取位置以提供转向播报和到达判断，导航结束即停止。本应用不申请「始终允许」定位权限，除导航外不在后台获取位置。

2. 相机、相册与麦克风

  • 何时收集：你主动拍摄工单现场照片或视频、从相册选择图片、扫描企业二维码时。
  • 为什么：作为工单的现场凭证，上传至你所属企业的服务器。录制视频时会一并录入现场声音。
  • 我们不会在你未主动触发的情况下访问相机、相册或麦克风。

3. 账号信息

  • 收集内容：登录用户名、所属企业（租户）、部门与角色。
  • 为什么：身份认证与权限控制。密码在传输前经过加密，我们不以明文存储或记录密码。

4. 设备与诊断信息

  • 通过高德地图 SDK 采集的设备标识与网络信息，用于地图与导航功能的正常运行。

二、第三方服务

本应用集成 高德地图 SDK（高德软件有限公司）以提供地图展示、定位与导航功能。高德会依其自身隐私政策处理相关数据，详见 https://lbs.amap.com/pages/privacy/。

除此之外，本应用不含任何第三方广告、数据分析或用户行为追踪 SDK。

三、数据如何被使用与共享

  • 你上传的数据仅传输至你所属企业部署的服务器，用于该企业的工单与网格业务。
  • 我们不向任何第三方出售或出租你的个人信息。
  • 我们不会将你的数据用于广告投放或跨应用追踪。

四、数据存储与保护

  • 业务数据存储于你所属企业指定的服务器。
  • 登录凭据在设备本地通过系统提供的安全存储保存（iOS 使用 Keychain，Android 使用 EncryptedSharedPreferences）。

五、你的权利

你可以随时在系统设置中关闭本应用的定位、相机、相册或麦克风权限；关闭后相关功能将不可用，但不影响其他功能的使用。如需查询、更正或删除你的个人信息，请联系你所属企业的管理员，或通过下方邮箱联系我们。

六、未成年人

本应用面向企业员工，不面向未满 14 周岁的未成年人，也不会主动收集其信息。

七、政策更新与联系方式

本政策如有更新，将在本页面公布。

Jingcang Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Jingcang is a work-order and field-grid management tool for enterprise employees. It is intended solely for use by employees authorized by their organization. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

Precise Location

  • When: while you use the map, browse nearby stores, check in on a work order, or enable location sharing as a field technician.
  • Why: to show your position on the map, filter stores and work orders by location, record visit timelines, and calculate navigation routes.
  • Background collection: only during in-app navigation. If you switch to another app or lock the screen while navigating, the app keeps accessing your location to provide turn-by-turn voice guidance and arrival detection, and stops as soon as navigation ends. The app does not request "Always" location permission and does not access location in the background for any other purpose.

Camera, Photo Library and Microphone

  • When: only when you actively take a photo or video for a work order, pick an image from your library, or scan a company QR code.
  • Why: to attach on-site evidence to work orders, uploaded to your organization's server. Video recording also captures ambient audio.
  • We never access the camera, photo library, or microphone without your explicit action.

Account Information

  • What: your login username, organization (tenant), department and role.
  • Why: authentication and access control. Passwords are encrypted before transmission; we do not store or log them in plain text.

Device and Diagnostic Information

  • Device identifiers and network information collected by the AMap SDK, required for map and navigation functionality.

2. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the AMap (Gaode) SDK by AutoNavi Software Co., Ltd. for maps, positioning and navigation. AMap processes data under its own privacy policy: https://lbs.amap.com/pages/privacy/.

Apart from this, the app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or user-tracking SDKs.

3. How Data Is Used and Shared

  • Data you submit is transmitted only to the server operated by your own organization.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

4. Storage and Protection

  • Business data is stored on servers designated by your organization.
  • Credentials are stored locally using platform-provided secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android EncryptedSharedPreferences).

5. Your Rights

You may revoke location, camera, photo library, or microphone permissions at any time in system settings. The related features will stop working, but the rest of the app remains usable. To access, correct, or delete your personal information, contact your organization's administrator or email us below.

6. Children

This app is intended for enterprise employees. It is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their information.

7. Updates and Contact

Any updates to this policy will be published on this page.
