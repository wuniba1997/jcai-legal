鲸舱 App 审核指引

本页面供 App 审核人员使用。「鲸舱」是面向企业内部员工的工单与网格管理工具， 首次启动需扫描企业二维码完成绑定，再用测试账号登录。

登录步骤

  1. 打开 App，在「绑定企业」页面点击「扫描二维码」；
  2. 用取景框对准下方二维码（建议在另一台设备或本页面打印件上显示）；
  3. 识别成功后进入登录页，输入 App Store Connect 审核信息中提供的演示账号登录。

For App Review (English)

This app is an internal work-order and field-grid management tool for enterprise employees. On first launch it requires scanning a company QR code to bind, then signing in with the demo account.

  1. Open the app. On the “Bind Company” screen, tap the scan button (扫描二维码).
  2. Point the camera at the QR code above (display this page on another device, e.g. a Mac or a second phone).
  3. After the tenant is bound (“demo”), sign in on the login screen with the demo account provided in the App Store Connect review information.
